As CTO of PARTNER BioResearch, I lead technical strategy and product engineering across platform architecture, AI/data workflows, integration planning, security, and user experience.

My work sits at the intersection of engineering and decision-making: define the right system, remove unnecessary complexity, make risk visible, and build enough structure that a team can move quickly without creating future debt.

I’m comfortable moving from architecture diagrams and data models into API contracts, interface states, production debugging, and executive conversations. The goal is not technology for its own sake — it is a product that works, scales responsibly, and is easier for the next person to understand.

Operating principle Make the complex legible. Make the important reliable.

How I work

Principles over buzzwords.
